# Part 1 — The breach (sequence)

Target: the bunker scan *Old Concrete Bunker PBR Scan* (Sketchfab `e8d5052a3a8e46c3a7eecd89d01cac7f`,
Pers Scans, CC-BY) half-buried at the foot of the cliffs, rocks piled around it, the ground around
it textured to match the scan's own ground, the inside of the bunker dark.

| # | Beat | Player | Teammates (Alpha, Bravo) | Camera / FX / sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wake up | Lying down, eyes open/close, blurred, gets up | Alpha kneels by the player, "Up! We're moving." | Black, tinnitus, muffled distant blasts, heartbeat, blur + vignette fade, slow head bob |
| 2 | Approach | Free to move (touch controls) | Walk to the bunker door, rifles low | Wind, sand gusts, far artillery |
| 3 | Plant | Objective: "Hold position" | Each kneels at one side of the door, plant animation, C4 sticks to the wall, red LED blinks | C4 beep loop, radio click |
| 4 | Ready | The detonator appears in front of the player; prompt "TAP TO DETONATE" over a pulsing button | Both stand, turn and look at the player, idle "waiting" pose | Subtle zoom towards the detonator |
| 5 | Press | First-person hands press the detonator (animation) | — | Click, the detonator's LED goes red |
| 6 | Countdown | Big 3 · 2 · 1 on screen | On "3" they sprint back from the door, vault/jump over the rocks, dive into cover | Beeps speed up with the count |
| 7 | Blast | — | Duck, cover their heads | Flash, fireball + smoke + debris flipbooks, flying rocks, dust wave, strong camera shake, ringing ears, muffled sound for ~2 s |
| 8 | Open | Objective: "Enter the bunker" | Get up, move to the opened entrance, wave the player in | Smoke drifting out of a dark opening, falling sand |

Nothing moves on from beat 4 until the player presses the detonator.

Assets (ready-made, free): bunker / C4 / detonator / soldiers from Sketchfab (CC-BY, needs
`SKETCHFAB_API_TOKEN`), animations from Quaternius Universal Animation Library (CC0) or Mixamo,
explosion flipbooks from Unity Labs (CC0), rocks and ground from Poly Haven (CC0), sounds from
OpenGameArt CC0 / Sonniss GDC bundles.
